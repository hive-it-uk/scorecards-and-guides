Good habits, whatever tool you use

Turning off training is only part of it. These habits protect you even when a setting changes without warning.

  • Keep secrets out

    Never paste passwords, access tokens or API keys into an AI tool. Keep them in a password manager, or in the environment variables your code reads when it runs.

  • Hide the files that matter

    Some tools let you list files they should never read. It’s called content exclusion, and it’s usually only on paid organisation plans. Check whether yours has it.

  • Use a work account for work

    For client work, use an account your organisation manages rather than a personal one. Organisation plans usually have stronger terms about training.

  • Don’t assume paid means private

    A paid plan isn’t automatically safer. JetBrains is a good example: its free non-commercial licence collects more of your data than its paid one, not less.

  • When in doubt, leave it out

    Would it cause a problem for you or a client if it leaked? Then keep it out of the tool.

  • Check again now and then

    Providers change these settings, and sometimes change the default. It’s worth a look every few months.
